Our Promise: Total Privacy

We want you to feel safe and happy. Here is our privacy promise in super simple words.

💻 Everything Stays on Your Device

PixelSlice Pro runs 100% inside your web browser. When you drop your pictures or sprite sheets here, they never leave your computer or phone. We do not have servers to save them because we don't need them!

1. No Accounts or Sign-ups

You never need to log in, create a password, or give us your name or email. Just open the page and start cutting your sprites right away.

2. Zero Ads and Zero Tracking

We don't show annoying pop-up ads, and we don't track what you do. Your creative work is completely private to you.

3. We Never Look at Your Images

Because everything is processed locally on your own screen, we cannot see, touch, download, or store your pictures. Your art belongs only to you.

Have fun creating awesome games and art!
